# Changelog

## Unreleased

- `--exclude STYLE` removes an animation from random selection and browsing, for example `--exclude accelerando` in terminals without the Accelerando font.
- Accelerando accepts opt-in `--bold`, independently of `--big` and `--symbols`, with a bundled Bold face for the native alien alphabet.
- Accelerando accepts `--big` for large solid-block glyphs, including matching block letters, digits and punctuation with `--symbols`, without changing terminal font size.
